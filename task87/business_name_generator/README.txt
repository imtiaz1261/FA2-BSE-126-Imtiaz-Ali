Business Name Generator

Usage:
1. Run: python business_name_generator.py
2. Enter the business type, e.g. "organic tea shop".
3. Enter the niche/target audience.
4. The program suggests 10 creative, available-sounding names with one-line reasoning.

Note: "available-sounding" does not mean legally or commercially available. Check domain, social handle, trademark, and local business-name registries before using a name.
